FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your stay at The Grand Luxe.

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM. Early check-in and late check-out are available upon request, subject to availability.
Do you offer airport transfers?
Yes, we offer private luxury airport transfers. Please contact our concierge team at least 24 hours in advance to arrange your pickup.
Is there a dress code for the restaurants?
Le Ciel and La Piazza maintain a smart-casual dress code. The Sky Lounge is more relaxed, but we kindly ask guests to avoid swimwear and athletic attire.
Are pets allowed at the hotel?
Yes, we are a pet-friendly hotel. We welcome small to medium-sized pets with a cleaning fee. Please inform us in advance so we can prepare a special welcome for your furry friend.
