About VitaCare

Compassionate care, backed by modern medicine

Started in 2010 as a small family practice in Indiranagar, VitaCare has grown into a multi-specialty centre that serves over 25,000 patients a year. Our specialists work as one team on a shared digital record, so you never have to repeat your history or carry a file of old reports.

To make high-quality, evidence-based healthcare accessible and affordable for every family, with honest advice and transparent pricing.
To be Bengaluru's most trusted neighbourhood health partner, where preventive care comes first and every patient feels heard.
Seva (service), integrity, clinical excellence, and respect for every patient's time, dignity and privacy.
