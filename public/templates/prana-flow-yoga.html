Our Story

A quiet place to come home to yourself

Prana Flow began in 2014 as a Sunday morning class on a borrowed Bengaluru rooftop: eight mats, one teacher and a thermos of adrak chai.

Our founder Meera learned yoga the old way, in a Mysore shala and on the banks of the Ganga in Rishikesh. Today we're a warm, wooden-floored studio with tall windows and a tulsi garden, but the spirit is the same. Yoga isn't about touching your toes. It's about what you learn on the way down.

प्राण (prana) means life force. Our only goal is to help you feel more of it, on the mat and long after you've rolled it up.
