FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about travelling to Kashmir.

Is Kashmir safe for tourists?
Kashmir is generally safe for tourists and welcomes thousands of visitors each year. The tourism infrastructure is well-developed, and locals are known for their hospitality. We recommend following standard travel precautions and staying informed about current conditions.
What is the best time to visit Kashmir?
April to October is ideal for green valleys, pleasant weather, and outdoor activities. December to March is perfect for snow lovers and skiing enthusiasts. Spring (March-April) is beautiful with blooming tulips.
How much does a Kashmir trip cost?
A 5-day trip typically costs between ₹15,000 to ₹35,000 per person depending on hotel category, vehicle type, and season. Budget trips start at ₹10,000, while luxury experiences can go up to ₹70,000+.
Can I customize my package?
Yes! All our packages are fully customizable. You can add or remove destinations, change hotel categories, select different vehicles, and adjust the duration to match your preferences.
Are cars with drivers available?
Yes, all our vehicles come with experienced local drivers who know the terrain well. Driver-inclusive options are standard for all packages and car rentals.
Can I book hotels separately?
Absolutely! You can book hotels independently through our hotel booking section. We offer everything from budget stays to luxury houseboats and 5-star resorts.
What documents are required?
Indian citizens need a valid government ID (Aadhaar, passport, or driver's license). Foreign nationals require a valid passport and Indian visa. Some areas near borders may need special permits.
How does booking work?
Simply choose your package, hotel, or car, fill in your details, and submit. Our team will contact you within 24 hours to confirm and provide payment options.
Can I modify my itinerary?
Yes! Even after booking, you can modify your itinerary with us subject to availability. We're flexible and want you to have the best possible experience.
Do you provide airport transfers?
Yes, we provide airport transfers from Srinagar International Airport as part of all our packages. You can also book standalone airport transfers.
