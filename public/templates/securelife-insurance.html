FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our insurance services.

01 How do I file an insurance claim?
You can file a claim directly through our online portal, mobile app, or by calling our 24/7 claims hotline. Simply provide your policy number and details about the incident. Our team typically processes claims within 24-48 hours.
02 Can I customize my insurance plan?
Absolutely! All of our plans are fully customizable. You can add or remove coverage options, adjust your deductible, and choose from various riders to create a plan that perfectly fits your needs.
03 How quickly can I get insured?
Most clients are fully covered within 24 hours of completing their application. The process is entirely online and takes less than 15 minutes. Your policy documents are sent to you immediately via email.
04 Do you offer discounts?
Yes! We offer multiple discount options including multi-policy discounts (up to 30%), safe driver discounts, loyalty rewards, and annual payment discounts. Ask your advisor about all available savings.
05 What happens if I miss a payment?
We offer a 30-day grace period for missed payments. Your coverage remains active during this time. If you need more flexibility, contact our support team to discuss payment arrangement options.
