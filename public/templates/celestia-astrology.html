FAQ

Questions, Answered

How accurate are online astrology consultations?

Accuracy depends on precise birth data and the astrologer's expertise. Our charts use Swiss Ephemeris calculations, and every astrologer is assessed on real case studies before joining. Most clients find their reading highly relevant, and if you're not satisfied with your first session we'll arrange a second opinion at no cost.

What if I don't know my exact time of birth?

You can still receive valuable insights from your Moon sign and planetary positions. For a precise ascendant, our astrologers offer Birth Time Rectification, which uses key life events to calculate your likely time of birth.

Is my personal information kept private?

Yes. Your birth details and conversations are encrypted and never shared with third parties. Astrologers see only the details needed for your reading, and you can delete your data from your account at any time.

How are consultations priced and paid for?

Services start from ₹499 per session. Chat and call consultations are also available per minute, with rates shown on each astrologer's profile. We accept UPI, cards, net banking and wallets, and your first 5 minutes with any astrologer are free.

Do you suggest gemstones or expensive remedies?

Our astrologers are trained to recommend simple, affordable remedies first, such as mantras, charity and lifestyle changes. Gemstones are suggested only when clearly indicated in your chart, and there is never any obligation to purchase.
