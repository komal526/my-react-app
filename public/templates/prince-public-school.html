Classes & Curriculum

Programs For Every Stage

Tailored learning journeys for each phase of a child's development.

Pre-Primary (Nursery - KG)

Phonics & Literacy Number Skills Creative Play Music & Movement

Our pre-primary program focuses on play-based learning that develops foundational literacy, numeracy, social skills, and motor abilities in a nurturing environment. Children learn through stories, songs, art, and guided exploration.

Primary (Classes 1 - 5)

English, Hindi, Maths Science Explorations Social Studies Physical Education

Primary years build strong academic foundations with an emphasis on reading, writing, numeracy, and inquiry-based learning. We foster curiosity and develop independent learning habits through interactive and hands-on activities.

Middle School (Classes 6 - 8)

Physics & Chemistry Biology Algebra & Geometry Computer Science

The middle school curriculum deepens subject knowledge while encouraging independent research, critical thinking, and collaborative projects. Students begin to discover their academic interests and passions.

Secondary (Classes 9 - 10)

CBSE Board Prep Science Stream Mathematics Social Sciences

Our secondary program prepares students for CBSE board examinations with rigorous academic coaching, regular assessments, and personalized mentoring. We focus on conceptual clarity and exam readiness.

Senior Secondary (Classes 11 - 12)

Science (PCM/PCB) Commerce Humanities Career Guidance

Senior secondary offers specialized streams with expert faculty, competitive exam preparation, and career counseling. We guide students toward their higher education goals with confidence.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, academics, and campus life.

What is the admission process for new students?
The admission process involves filling out an enquiry form, visiting the campus, submitting the application with required documents (birth certificate, previous school records, photographs), and a brief interaction with the student and parents. Admissions are confirmed based on seat availability and eligibility criteria.
What curriculum does Prince Public School follow?
We follow the CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education) curriculum from Pre-Primary through Senior Secondary. Our curriculum is enriched with experiential learning, STEM education, digital resources, and a wide range of co-curricular activities.
What are the school timings?
School timings are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM, and Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM (for co-curricular activities and enrichment programs). Pre-Primary students have slightly shorter hours.
Is transportation available?
Yes, we provide safe and reliable bus transportation across major routes in the city. Our buses are equipped with GPS tracking, CCTV cameras, and trained attendants. Transport fees are additional and based on distance.
What co-curricular activities are available?
We offer a wide range of activities including sports (football, cricket, basketball, athletics), performing arts (music, dance, drama), visual arts, debate club, science club, technology club, and more. Every student is encouraged to participate in at least one co-curricular activity.
What documents are required for admission?
Required documents include: birth certificate, previous school report card and transfer certificate (if applicable), passport-size photographs of student and parents, and address proof. Original documents should be presented for verification at the time of admission.
