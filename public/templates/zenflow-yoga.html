About ZenFlow

A Sanctuary for Body & Mind

Since day one, ZenFlow Yoga Center has been dedicated to helping people reconnect with themselves through the ancient practice of yoga. Our light-filled studios, expert guidance, and warm community make every visit feel like coming home.

We believe yoga is for everybody — regardless of age, flexibility, or experience. Come as you are, and leave a little lighter.

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need prior yoga experience? +

Not at all. We offer classes for every level, and our beginner sessions are designed to welcome you at your own pace.

What should I bring to class? +

Just comfortable clothing and water. Mats and props are provided free with every membership.

Can I cancel my membership anytime? +

Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract required.

Do you offer private sessions? +

Yes, private sessions are available and included in our Premium plan, or bookable separately.

Is prenatal yoga safe for all trimesters? +

Our prenatal classes are guided by certified instructors and adapted to each stage of pregnancy — always consult your doctor first.
